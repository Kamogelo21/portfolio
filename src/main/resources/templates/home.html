About Me

I am from a small township in Soweto called Rockville. I did not know anything about Software Engineering until I got to WeThinkCode. A lot of my love for building software started there and I learnt a lot from the institution. From independent working to group collaborations, and peer-to-peer learning, I inherited resilience and confidence to chanel myself into real, industry work. Towards the end of the program I started utilizing my skills through building applications for small businesses around me. That was my first step into the industry, and it brought me joy to see the applications alive and bringing more revenue as well as ease into the businesses. That then got me to open an account in Upwork and expand my love for software into freelance work. I am always seeking for growth in the industry and I cannot wait to learn and contribute to big IT firms, and perhaps start my own in the future.
